About

My thesis, supervised by Prof. Michael Lipsett, is a compliant revolute joint for a soil surface sampler. Before that I was mechanical lead on McGill Robotics' autonomous underwater vehicle team, where I revived an inherited vehicle and rebuilt its frame for competition, and I wrote motor control firmware on STM32 and Teensy. I like work that sits between mechanical hardware and the code that runs it.
